# Checks on the watch

What the emulators cannot show, one line each, for a run on the owner's watch. The first seven lines follow the v3 spec's list "Not possible to verify without the watch" in its order (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-wear-compass-v3-watch-report.md`); the rest are the other things the tasks left for the watch.

## Before

- Build: `./gradlew assembleWatch` writes `app/build/outputs/apk/watch/app-watch.apk` (minified like a release, signed with this machine's debug key). `./gradlew assembleDebug` writes `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`. Both have the check screen; the release build does not.
- Install on the watch with its own serial (`~/Library/Android/sdk/platform-tools/adb devices` lists it): `~/Library/Android/sdk/platform-tools/adb -s <watch serial> install -r app/build/outputs/apk/watch/app-watch.apk`. It replaces a debug build in place and keeps its data. The scripts under `scripts/` that talk to a device refuse to run while the watch is attached; they are for the emulators only. The ones that talk to no device run whatever is attached: `scripts/relevant-tests.sh`, `scripts/readout-gaps.py`, `scripts/test-counts.sh` (it reads result files), `scripts/instrument-results.py`, `scripts/deadline.sh`, and `scripts/quick-tests.sh` (or `scripts/run-test.sh`) given unit test classes only.
- The check screen: settings, `Calibrate`, then the `Check` row at the bottom of calibration. It keeps the display on while it is open.
- Smoothness is judged on the watch build; the debug build is not optimized.

## The checks

| # | What to check | How | What to look at on the check screen |
|-|-|-|-|
| 1 | Smoothness | With the watch build: swipe compass, waypoints, settings and back a few times; scroll the settings list to the end and back with a finger and with the crown. Note any stutter. | Nothing; this is by eye. |
| 2 | Drift, and which heading source cures it | Stand in one place, note where north points, turn around in place five times one way and five the other, and note where north points after each. Do it once with each source: the first row switches the compass to `Fused`, `Magnetic` or `Blend` for the running app (back to `Fused` at each start). | `Heading source` (the one in use). At rest, `Fused heading`, `Magnetic heading` and `Blend heading` should agree within a few degrees; after the turns, how far `Fused heading` has moved from `Magnetic heading`. |
| 3 | The three dial vibrations: whether the watch supports the primitives, and their feel | Tap `Play thin line`, `Play thick line` and `Play target`. Then on the compass, with `Tick feedback` on and a target focused, turn slowly past fine ticks, a heavier tick (every 30 degrees) and the target's direction. They should feel light, deeper, and a double knock in between. | Under `Vibrations`: `Yes` or `No` for each of the eight primitives; under each `Play` row, `Primitives` or `Constant` (the way it plays on this watch). |
| 4 | Whether the watch gives a color scheme | Settings, `Colors`: `Watch face` is offered, or `Not available on this watch` shows. With `Watch face` chosen, change the watch face's colors and come back. Optional, read only: `~/Library/Android/sdk/platform-tools/adb -s <watch serial> shell settings get global dynamic_color_theme_enabled` (the setting the Wear library reads; 1 is on). | Under `Watch colors`: the six colors with their dots, or `Color scheme` `None`. They are read again each time the screen comes back. |
| 5 | Whether the double pinch arrives | On the compass, at rest and in view, pinch thumb and forefinger twice. The library gives a vibration of its own; the status should read `Saved Waypoint N`. | Under `Inputs`: `One-handed gestures` (`Yes` means the system says it detects them), `Wear SDK`, `Wear SDK reached`. |
| 6 | Whether calibration is now usable | Settings, `Calibrate`. Turn the wrist slowly in every direction. The ring's segments should light, `Covered` should rise, and the screen should stay on for two minutes from the opening or from the last segment that lit, whichever is later. Note whether the ring (0.21 of the screen's radius) can be read while the wrist turns. | `Level` and `Field` (microtesla; calibration warns below 25 or above 65); `Error` (see 9). |
| 7 | Battery with location running while the app is open | Note the battery percent, keep the compass on screen (location runs while the compass or the waypoints screen is open) for 30 minutes, note it again. Do the same with the app closed for comparison. | Nothing. |
| 8 | The side button | Only where the watch describes a button an app can use. On the compass a single press saves, as a double tap does (with `Breadcrumb trail` on, 300 ms after it is let go, when no second press follows); with `Breadcrumb trail` on, a double press starts a trail. Where it describes none, a press does what the system does with it. | Under `Inputs`: `Buttons`, `Side keys` (`STEM_1` to `STEM_3`, or `None`), `Assignable button` (`Yes` or `No`, the decision the side button follows). |
| 9 | The estimated heading error | Look at the `Error` value at once when calibration or the check screen opens, then for a minute. `--` means the watch gave no estimate (the app takes an estimate of 0, which the watch reports when it has none, as none). If it ever shows more than 30 degrees, the compass should show `Calibrate`, and keep it until the error is below 25 degrees. | `Error`: `±N°` or `--`, at the sensor's start and after. |
| 10 | Whether the watch can work out the magnetic heading | Tap the first row until it has been each of the three. | `Heading source`: `Magnetic` and `Blend` when the watch can; `Fused` with `Magnetic unavailable` or `Blend unavailable` under it when it cannot. |
| 11 | The check screen keeps the display on | Leave the check screen open without touching it. The display should stay on for two minutes from the opening, after which the watch's own screen timeout applies; a touch or a turn of the crown counts the two minutes again. | The whole screen. |
| 12 | The icon | In the launcher: the disc `#153D70` fills the round mask, beside the system's icons; whether the disc shows against the launcher's black (their contrast is 1.93 to 1). Where the watch offers themed icons, turn them on: the monochrome layer (ring and needle) should be drawn in the theme's colors. Open the app: the start screen shows the icon with its disc on black. | Nothing. |
| 13 | The hold-flat drawing on the smaller watch | On a 41 mm watch, with `Show coordinates` on and a target about 3 miles away, tilt the watch upright so `Hold flat` and its drawing show. On the small round emulator (384 x 384 px), the stand-in for the smaller watch, the turning drawing comes within 1.4 px of `3.1 mi` under it; how the 41 mm watch's screen compares with that emulator's was not measured. Look whether they touch. | Nothing. |
| 14 | Calibration's ring is kept | Open calibration, light a few segments, leave, close the app (swipe it away), open calibration again: the same segments are lit and `Covered` shows the same share. They start over only when `Level` has fallen to `Low` or `Unreliable`; note whether that happens without a reason (for example at each start of the app). | `Level`. |
| 15 | No pointer when the position is uncertain | Indoors (where fixes are poor), start a trail, drop a crumb, step a few meters away and tap `Trail` to retrace. While the crumb is nearer than the fix's accuracy there should be no line or arrow for it, and the distance should read like `10 ft ±50 ft`; the crumb should stay. Outdoors, walk 30 m or more away: the line should come back and point at the crumb. | Nothing. |
| 16 | Watch face colors with a gray scheme | With the watch face whose scheme the app read on 2026-10-16 (primary `#FFF9EB`), choose `Colors` `Watch face`. North should be a deep red near the watch face's own (`#FF4D33`) and the reddest color on the screen, the pointer and the words that can be tapped a pale gray-red (`#FFC4B2`), the ink a warm white; new waypoints grays and gray-reds; the error color (a delete's ring, a failed save) a lighter red (`#FF7C65`). Focus a waypoint whose bearing is near the pointer, or near north, and tell the pointer, north and the focused waypoint's arrow apart at a glance; note any pair that is hard to tell apart. | Under `Watch colors`: the six colors; `Primary` should still be `#FFF9EB`. |
